Labyrinth

Birthday Edition

Somewhere in these shifting corridors, people who love you have hidden their voices. Find every treasure, then make your way home.

  1. Your card shows the treasure you must reach — it glows on the board.
  2. Every turn you must slide the spare tile into the maze at one of the gold arrows. You cannot push it straight back where it just came out.
  3. Then walk as far as the open corridors allow, or stay where you are.
  4. Reach the treasure and a message unlocks. Then a rival shoves the spare tile in somewhere of its own choosing.
  5. Collect them all and return to your corner to win.
